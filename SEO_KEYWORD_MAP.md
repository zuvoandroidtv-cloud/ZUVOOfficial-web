## 2. Cannibalization Prevention Policy

* Each model size is isolated to its distinct URL path (`/products/zuvo-XX-...`).
* The collection page `/products` targets broad discovery and category terms without competing for individual model long-tail queries.
* The `/dealership` page strictly targets B2B wholesale and dealership commercial queries, leaving consumer purchase queries to `/products` and `/contact`.
* The `/warranty` page strictly targets service, coverage, and repair queries, differentiating it from general pre-purchase customer service on `/contact`.
